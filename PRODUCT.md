# Product

## Users

Tend is for people who want help remembering recurring parts of real life without adopting a productivity system. They may open the app once or twice a week, often in a quiet maintenance mindset: checking what needs care, marking something tended, or adding a recurring item before it drifts out of memory.

## Product Purpose

Tend tracks recurring life-maintenance items across household care, health upkeep, relationships, pets, vehicles, personal routines, and life admin. Its core job is to answer: what parts of my life could use attention now? Success means users can add an item quickly, understand why it surfaced, and act without guilt-driven overdue language.

## Brand Personality

Calm, honest, useful. Tend should feel like a quiet kitchen counter note: warm, clear, and human, with enough structure to be reliable and no pressure mechanics.

## Anti-references

Tend should not look or behave like a kanban board, task manager, gamified habit tracker, alarm dashboard, or corporate productivity suite. Avoid red overdue states, streaks, points, progress rings, dense analytics, nag loops, harsh validation, and marketing gloss.

## Design Principles

- Awareness, not pressure: surface drift with neutral language and gentle emphasis.
- Fast to scan: make status, type, rhythm, and last tended easy to understand at a glance.
- Sparse musts: reserve stronger treatment for items that truly cannot drift.
- Honest metadata: show why something is appearing instead of hiding the logic.
- Low-friction capture: adding a recurring item should take under a minute.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target accessible product UI with readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, clear labels, and reduced-motion support. Status must not rely on color alone, and copy should avoid shame, urgency theater, or punitive phrasing.
